## Project title
**Rift Runners: Nexus**

## Short description
**Rift Runners: Nexus** is a live 2–8 player browser arena built for instant cross-device play. Players join with a six-character code or QR link, collect energy, and bank it at the central Nexus before the 90-second clock expires. Carrying more energy creates a deliberate risk/reward tradeoff: a full four-item load earns a +2 bonus but slows the runner, while a timed Pulse ability can knock valuable cargo loose from nearby rivals. Mid-round Prism Rifts introduce 3-point objectives, and temporary Overdrive and Shield pickups create tactical swings. The game is server-authoritative, supports desktop and touch controls, handles reconnects and late-join spectators, and requires no player account or install.

## What makes it distinctive
The core decision is not simply “collect the most.” Players continuously choose between banking safely or carrying a larger, slower load for a bonus. Because Pulse can force nearby rivals to drop their highest-value cargo, every trip back to the Nexus becomes a small multiplayer risk calculation. Timed Prism Rifts pull everyone toward the same conflict point and keep the 90-second round changing.

## 45-second judge demo
1. Device A opens the URL and creates a room.
2. Device B scans the lobby QR code and joins from a phone.
3. Device A starts the round.
4. Both devices move simultaneously in the same arena.
5. One player collects 4 shards and banks them to show the +2 Full Load bonus.
6. Wait for a Prism Rift, collect the gold 3-point Prism, then use Pulse on the other player so dropped cargo appears live on both screens.
7. Let the clock finish and show the synchronized results / highlights screen.
