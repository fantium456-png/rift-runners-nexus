How to play Rift Runners

  1. Collect. Run over cyan shards. You can carry four. A gold Prism is worth 3 points.
  2. Bank. Touch the central Nexus to convert your cargo into permanent score. Four cargo items at once earns +2.
  3. Attack. Press Space on desktop or tap PULSE on mobile. Nearby rivals can drop their most valuable cargo. Pulse recharges in 6 seconds.
  4. Use powerups. Green Overdrive boosts speed. Purple Shield blocks one Pulse while active.
  5. Win. Highest banked score after 90 seconds wins. Anything still in your cargo at the buzzer is worth zero.
High-risk play: a full four-item load earns +2, but every carried item slows you slightly.
